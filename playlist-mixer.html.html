Playlist Mixer

Paste your playlist link and press Load playlist. The playlist must be public or unlisted.
iPhone and iPad do not let web pages change a video's volume, and they may stop one deck when the other starts. The crossfader is hidden here. Hand off is a quick cut: it starts the other deck at its cue point and stops this one. Turn on Auto-advance to have the next track load and start by itself. Tap Hand off once yourself first, so your iPhone lets deck B start on its own. Keep the screen on, because locking it stops playback.
You opened this as a file. If a deck shows a video error, run python3 -m http.server 8000 in this folder and open http://localhost:8000/playlist-mixer.html instead.
